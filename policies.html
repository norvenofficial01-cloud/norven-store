Policies

Privacy

Customer data is intended to be visible only to the customer and authorized store administration when connected to Supabase RLS.

Terms

Orders are subject to product availability, payment verification and applicable laws.

Shipping

Shipping rules are configurable from Admin. Third-party courier status can be integrated later.

Return & Refund

Customer return/refund controls are currently disabled. The admin dashboard contains a future-ready section for later activation.
